## Physical producer split

The function was historically embedded in the remaining `th04_main.asm`
CIRCLE_TEXT contribution. A direct source replacement would therefore disturb
subsequent accepted owners unless the surrounding layout is preserved.

The final replay layout is:

```text
0AAF:11A4  0468  th04_main.asm
0AAF:160C  004B  th04/itemsinv.cpp
0AAF:1657  0001  th04\itemgap185.asm
0AAF:1658  0502  th04\cirsuf185.asm
0AAF:1B5A  0101  th04/circle.cpp
```

`itemgap185.asm` is generated by a hash-bound extraction of the actual NOP
following the historical function ENDP. It is replay plumbing and receives zero
reconstruction credit. `cirsuf185.asm` is also replay-only target-derived
scaffold, beginning after that NOP and retaining word segment alignment. The
maintained C++ owner receives credit only for the 75 function bytes.

This three-owner split matters. A word-aligned suffix without the gap caused
TLINK to insert `00` at load `0xC147` and shifted the source NOP to `0xC148`. A
byte-aligned suffix placed the NOP correctly but changed internal TASM `EVEN`
parity and shifted downstream CIRCLE and later segment layout. The source-extracted
one-byte gap followed by a word-aligned suffix preserves both the target NOP and
all later public addresses.

The replay suffix also replays two already-accepted aggregate-only symbol
adaptations: the Mugetsu/Gengetsu backdrop public truncation and the
`CLEAR_DWORDS` alias. A fail-closed optional transform hands off the three GRCG
publics when the v171 aggregate transform is active while preserving the
`PLAYFIELD_FILLM_0_40_384_274` public still owned by `th04_main.asm`.
