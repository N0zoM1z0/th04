## Logical function extents

The target has two public entries, not one conventional compiler function:

- `pointnums_add_yellow()` executes a 0x1A-byte entry stub at load
  `0x13D90..0x13DA9`, jumps over the white entry, and then executes the shared
  0x33-byte tail at `0x13DBE..0x13DF0`. Its logical body is therefore 0x4D
  bytes over a 0x61-byte span.
- `pointnums_add_white()` is contiguous from load `0x13DAA` through the same
  `RET 6` at `0x13DF0`, for 0x47 bytes.

Fresh target decoding and the pinned TASM listing agree that the shared tail
selects the 16-byte slot, marks it alive, and only then executes
`PUSH BP; MOV BP,SP` before reading the three Pascal stack arguments. This is
why Ghidra's 0x1A yellow observation cannot be accepted as a complete function.

## Source-language/origin decision

v134/v135 already tested ordinary dual C++, a BX-pseudoregister white body,
`-O/-O-`, `-k/-k-`, `-G/-G-`, and out-of-line common-helper forms. Those probes
do not generate the target's sibling public entries converging on a delayed
shared BP frame. They remain negative compiler evidence rather than proof by
themselves.

v139 adds independent original-target evidence from the registered private
`th05-main-smoke` image (SHA-256
`c41f6e6b9a97b2433acc576ceaee800707c8d4ea7e498150a259663c8fa7d4f0`).
An operand-wildcarded opcode/control-flow signature has exactly one TH05
load-module hit, at `0x16E28`. That target independently preserves the same
architecture: yellow entry, white public entry, jump/fallthrough into one
shared tail, `SHL BX,4`, slot initialization, and delayed `PUSH BP; MOV BP,SP`,
ending in `RET 6`. The TH05-specific yellow count and tail fields differ where
the game semantics require them to differ.

This target-to-target corroboration is independent of ReC98. ReC98's shared
TH04/TH05 assembly scaffold remains useful historical reconstruction evidence,
but is not being used to authenticate itself. Combined with the failed natural
TC4J matrices and the exact symbolic TASM producer below, the two entries are
reclassified from `authored/reconstruct` to `original-asm/attest-asm`.
